Frequently Asked Questions

What is JAC Delhi counseling? +
JAC (Joint Admission Counseling) Delhi is the centralized counseling process for admission to Delhi's top engineering colleges — DTU, NSUT, IIITD, IGDTUW, and others — based on JEE Main ranks.
How many rounds are there in JAC Delhi? +
Typically 3–4 rounds. Round 1 and 2 are the primary rounds. Round 3 and Spot Round fill remaining seats. After each round, you can upgrade or stay with your current seat.
What documents are needed for document verification? +
You need your JEE Scorecard, Class 10 & 12 marksheets, Aadhar, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), and passport size photos. Check our checklist above for the full list.
Can I upgrade my seat in later rounds? +
Yes! After Round 1, you can opt for "Upgrade" during subsequent rounds. If your preferred college/branch becomes available, your seat gets upgraded automatically based on your preference list order.
Is there a fee during registration? +
Yes, a registration fee is charged (typically ₹1,000–₹2,000). Additionally, a seat acceptance fee is required once a seat is allotted. Refund policies apply if you withdraw before deadlines.

Delhi Technological University

📍 Rohini, Delhi 🏅 NIRF 30

Known for its legacy and strong engineering culture, DTU blends rigorous academics with one of North India's most vibrant campuses. Coding, core tech and startups thrive here, backed by excellent alumni reach. Massive fests like Engifest, solid placements and a competitive yet balanced environment make it a top choice for holistic growth.

📍 Dwarka, Delhi 🏅 NIRF 70

NSUT offers a disciplined academic environment with high placement consistency, especially in tech roles. The campus is modern and structured, with active societies and growing research culture. Known for coding and corporate exposure, it strikes a balance between academics and extracurriculars, though relatively stricter than peers.

📍 Okhla, Delhi 🏅 NIRF 63

IIIT-Delhi is research-driven and academically intense, focusing on computer science, AI, and interdisciplinary tech. Smaller batches ensure strong faculty interaction and cutting-edge labs. With a focus on inclusivity and growth, it's a strong option for women aiming for both academics and career opportunities.

Get Ready

College Prep

What to expect, what to avoid, and how to hit the ground running.

🗺️

Your First Year Roadmap

Month 1–2: Orientation

Get your ID card, join societies, attend all induction events. Don't skip.

Month 3–4: Foundation

Focus on basic programming (C, Python), Mathematics, and DSA fundamentals.

Month 5–6: Build

Start your first project. Join a technical club. Begin competitive programming.

Month 7–8: Grow

Apply for your first internship (off-campus). Build your GitHub profile.

🎓

What to Expect in College

  • Freedom you've never had — handle it wisely
  • Huge skill gap between you and seniors — that's normal
  • Grades matter less than projects + skills for tech roles
  • CGPA matters for core/govt/MBA/higher studies
  • The first 2 semesters set your GPA trend — don't slip
  • Network relentlessly — your batchmates are future colleagues
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping classes thinking "I'll study later"
  • Not joining any society or club in Sem 1
  • Waiting for placements to start coding
  • Comparing yourself to everyone on LinkedIn
  • Neglecting physical and mental health
  • Underestimating internship season deadlines
💡

Skills to Start Now

Python DSA Git & GitHub Linux Basics SQL HTML/CSS Problem Solving Communication

Start these before your first semester even begins. You'll thank yourself later.
